Our Story & Clinical Mission

About Ojas Physiotherapy

Empowering Movement. Restoring Confidence.

At Ojas Physiotherapy, we believe that movement is medicine. Founded with a mission to bring compassionate, hospital-grade physiotherapy care closer to the Lucknow community, we are dedicated to helping patients of all ages live pain-free, active, and fulfilling lives.

Meet Our Doctors Explore Services

Our Philosophy

We don't just treat symptoms. We heal people.

Our treatment methodology combines evidence-based medicine with heartfelt human empathy. We understand that pain affects sleep, work productivity, and emotional well-being. That is why our senior practitioners, Dr. Arpita Singh and Dr. Muntaha Jafri, address root-cause biomechanics rather than just temporary surface pain.

Whether it is in-home rehabilitation for an elderly stroke survivor or sports conditioning for an injured runner, every treatment plan is 100% personalized to your lifestyle and recovery timeline.
