Patient Privacy Guarantee

How We Safeguard Your Information

At Aarogya Physiotherapy, we adhere to strict medical confidentiality and ethical data standards:

  • Medical Confidentiality: Your clinical history, diagnosis, and recovery records are strictly restricted to your treating physiotherapist.
  • Communication Use Only: Your phone number and WhatsApp are used solely to share appointment reminders, exercise videos, and recovery tips.
  • Zero Third-Party Sharing: We never sell, lease, or distribute patient contact details to advertisers or third parties.
  • Patient Data Rights: You may request full access to your digital case notes or complete erasure from our system at any time.
